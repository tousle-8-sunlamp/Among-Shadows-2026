## 🕹️ About

Among Shadows is an asymmetric PvP fantasy social-deduction game developed and published by TMXN Games. Players enter blighted lands as members of the Order of the Secret Fire, battling monsters and investigating infection sources while hidden Underworld Apostles work to sabotage the mission. The game combines real-time combat, cooperation, deception, and survival objectives, with a planned release on September 24, 2026.

Among Shadows is for players who enjoy cooperative action, fantasy combat, survival pressure, and social deduction with hidden enemies.

## ✨ Features

- 🕵️ **Asymmetric Social Deduction** — Identify hidden traitors while Underworld Apostles deceive and sabotage the Order.
- ⚔️ **Fast-Paced Combat** — Battle undead, bandits, fanatics, and powerful wraiths with swords and magic.
- 🌑 **Blood Moon Evacuation** — Protect the convoy and Lord during a desperate escape through enemy-infested lands.
- 🦠 **Infection Hunt** — Investigate corrupted locations and destroy the sources spreading the infection.
- 🧰 **Gather and Upgrade** — Collect materials, solve mechanisms, forge stronger weapons, and learn new magic.
- 🔥 **Unique Abilities** — Play Envoys with distinct combat techniques, magical abilities, and strategic roles.
- 🤝 **Online Multiplayer** — Coordinate with allies in online PvP and cooperative fantasy battles.
